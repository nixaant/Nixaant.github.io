I'm Tendou Hiro — 18 years old, self-taught, and fully committed to the path of manga.

Before this, I spent 4+ years as a freelance 2D animator on Fiverr. I got good at it. But animation is a team sport, and I've always been a solo person. That tension wore me down until I couldn't ignore it anymore.

Choosing manga meant starting over in a lot of ways — learning story structure, panel composition, pacing. Skills I'd spent years building suddenly didn't apply. It was humbling. It still is sometimes.

But one thing carried over completely: years of life drawing practice I never stopped doing. That foundation is why my art feels grounded. Real weight, real anatomy, real gravity — that part came with me.

Now I draw every day and I'm not looking back.
